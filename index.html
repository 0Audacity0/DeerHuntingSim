  • Deer are scarce by day. At night there are about 1.4x as many, but it is quite dark.
  • Walking and running spook deer from far away. Stand still to get closer.
  • Gunshots are loud, meaning everything nearby will hear it, including the gray one.
  • The gray one lives in the gray biome. If it chases you, press E at a bush to hide.
  • Bear traps are inside the gray biome. Mash WASD to break free. Mud slows you down.
  • You can carry 2 deer, but they slow you down a lot. Sell them at the shop next to the cabin for $10 each.
  • Save up $70 for the axe. Chop down a tree with a deer on top and the deer comes down with it.
  • The gray one takes 5 shotgun hits to kill, and it runs faster than you.
  • On mobile, the left stick moves (push past the ring to sprint) and dragging anywhere else looks around.
