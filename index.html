  • Deer are scarce by day. At night there are about 1.4x as many, but it is quite dark.
  • Walking and running spook deer from far away. Stand still to get closer.
  • Gunshots are loud, meaning everything nearby will hear it, including the gray one.
  • The gray one lives in the gray biome. If it chases you, press E at a bush to hide.
  • Bear traps are inside the gray biome. Mash WASD to break free. Mud slows you down.
  • You can carry 2 deer, but they slow you down a lot. Sell them at the shop by the road east of the cabin (the arrow in the bottom left points to it) for $10 each.
  • Save up $70 for the axe. Chop down a tree with a deer on top and the deer comes down with it.
  • The gray one takes 5 shotgun hits to kill, and it runs faster than you. If it catches you, you die instantly.
  • You always have 3 hearts. Bears are rare and charge you when they spot you. Every swipe costs 1 heart. A bear takes 8 rifle shots or 6 shotgun shots, takes both hands to carry and sells for $20.
  • Rabbits are small, fast and jumpy. They sell for $5 each.
  • You can even carry the dead gray one (both hands, so you move like you carry 2 deer) and sell it for $30. Check the Quests tab in the shop for extra cash.
  • The radar ($60) shows animals around you: orange is deer, pale is rabbits and red is bears. Bandages ($15, key H) heal 1 heart, and sleeping in the bed heals all of them.
  • Bear repellent ($25, key V) makes a cloud that scares bears away for about 35 seconds. Rain and fog make animals notice you less, but you can see less too.
  • The bar at the bottom shows your items. Pick the difficulty when starting a new run.
  • On mobile, the left stick moves (push past the ring to sprint) and dragging anywhere else looks around.
